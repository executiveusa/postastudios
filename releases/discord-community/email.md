# I opened a Discord for the PostaStudios community

Hey, it's Paulo. 👋

I built PostaStudios because I couldn't stay consistent posting. Being a developer, I gave it an MCP server, and made it open source: AGPL, self-hostable, no seat tax, no feature gates.

So I opened a Discord too.

## What you'll find there

You'll find people running PostaStudios self-hosted, and people paying for cloud, in the same room. Talking, helping each other out, sharing what they've built and what's breaking. That feedback is basically how I decide what to build next.

Come say hi 👋
